Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about the application.

What is the minimum loan amount?

The minimum loan amount shown on this website is KSh 5,000.

What is the maximum loan amount?

The maximum loan amount shown on this website is KSh 250,000.

What is the application/service fee?

The stated application/service fee is 7% of the requested loan amount. For example, a KSh 10,000 request has a KSh 700 fee, while a KSh 50,000 request has a KSh 3,500 fee.

What is the repayment period?

The repayment period stated on this website is 9 months. Final applicable terms should be confirmed during processing.

Does submitting the application guarantee approval?

No. Submission of an application does not guarantee approval. An application is first received and remains pending final processing and applicable assessment.

Which types of loans can I apply for?

You can apply under Personal, Business, School Fees or Farmer loan categories.

Can I apply if I already have another loan?

The application form allows you to disclose an existing loan. Any effect of an existing loan on an application is subject to the applicable assessment and processing.

How will I know what happens after I apply?

Your application is received for processing. The contact information you provide can be used to communicate with you regarding your application.
