Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the application.

What is the minimum loan amount?

The displayed minimum is KSh 5,000.

What is the maximum loan amount?

The displayed maximum is KSh 250,000.

What is the stated application/service fee?

The website displays a stated fee of 7% of the requested amount. Any fee that is actually payable should be clearly disclosed and agreed before payment.

What repayment period is displayed?

The website currently displays a stated repayment period of 3 months. Final contractual terms should be confirmed in writing.

Does applying guarantee approval?

No. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval or disbursement.

Can I apply through WhatsApp?

Yes. The WhatsApp button opens a direct chat with the contact number displayed on this website.

Should I send my M-PESA PIN or OTP?

No. Never send your M-PESA PIN, OTP, password or other security credentials to anyone.
